# Sunrise Coffee Chain: findings for the CCO (2023-2024)

Generated by `run_analysis.py`. P&L figures come from `fact_store_monthly_profitability` only. `fact_daily_sales` is a ~20% sample and is used only for shares, ratios and within-store comparisons. Medians are shown with means; groups with fewer than 5 stores or 30 rows are flagged **small sample**.

## 1. Data quality

7 high, 12 medium and 13 low severity issues. Full list in `outputs/data_quality.md`.

- **Store formats present** (dim_store): {'High-Street': 58, 'Transport-Hub': 2}; no Retail-Park stores at all
- **Transport-Hub coverage** (dim_store): 2 stores, both ['Luton'] ['Train-Station']
- **store_status vs trading** (dim_store): {'Active': 57, 'Temporarily-Closed': 2, 'Refurbishing': 1}; no Closed-Permanently. Non-active stores still report revenue in all months: {19: 24, 28: 24, 52: 24}
- **Coverage** (fact_daily_sales): 8,910 distinct store-days of 43,800 possible (20.3%); 149-194 rows per store
- **Grain / duplicates** (fact_daily_sales): 1,090 extra rows; 2,096 rows share a store-day with conflicting revenue and top product
- **Daily sample vs monthly P&L** (both facts): Store-level correlation of daily mean revenue with monthly revenue is only r=0.24; daily footfall is a median 76% of monthly avg_daily_footfall
- **profitability_status vs margin** (monthly): 600 of 1440 rows (42%) disagree with the documented bands; label ranges overlap (Profitable 10.3% to 33.5%, Break-Even -4.6% to 17.6%)
